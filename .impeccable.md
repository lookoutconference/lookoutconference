## Design Context

### Users
- **Academics and presenters** from many disciplines (history, architecture, forestry, literature, art, and more). They come for the annual theme, the call for abstracts, deadlines, and the program. They read carefully and need dates and requirements to be easy to find.
- **Archive browsers** who look up past sessions (panel pages, session videos, proceedings, booklets). They are researching or catching up, so they need clear structure and findable content.
- **Locals and the public** from Moscow and the Palouse who check out events at the University of Idaho campus, the 1912 Center, and Hunga Dunga Brewing. They want the when, the where, and whether they can come.

The site is a one-day conference (next one March 20, 2027) hosted by the University of Idaho (CDIL / Library). It is built with Jekyll on a Minimal Mistakes base, which is currently the stock default skin.

### Brand Personality
**Scholarly, rugged, generous.**
- *Scholarly:* a serious interdisciplinary conference with the rigor of a journal or proceedings volume.
- *Rugged:* grounded in the backcountry through lookout towers, fire, weather, and distance. The ruggedness should come from the material and the photography, not from costume.
- *Generous:* open to academics and passion projects alike (podcasters, restorers, photo collectors). The tone is welcoming and plainspoken, never gatekeeping.

Emotional goals: credibility, curiosity, and a sense of place.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme:** light. The site is read in daytime, often at length, so it needs comfortable reading contrast.
- **Identity:** free to diverge from the University of Idaho brand. NILC should have its own identity, with university and partner logos credited rather than dominant.
- **Assets to lean on:** strong landscape and tower photography (Trinity Peak, Sheep Hill, St. Mary's, Diablo, Spot Theta) and the firetower-as-letter logo.
- **Anti-references:**
  - *Rustic kitsch:* fake wood grain, distressed "camp" fonts, Smokey Bear clip art, or merit-badge styling.
  - *Tech/startup:* SaaS gradients, dark UI with glowing accents, glassmorphism, or grids of generic icon cards.
- **Palette direction:** derive it from the actual subject, such as the tones of the landscape and the lookout's working materials. Tint neutrals toward the chosen brand hue, keep the accent rare, and use no pure black or white.

### Design Principles
1. **Content is the landscape.** Let the photography and the ideas carry the page, and use chrome that recedes. Structure should come from editorial hierarchy (type, rule lines, whitespace), not from boxes and cards.
2. **Scholarly legibility first.** Use a generous reading measure (65–75ch), a clear type scale, and make dates, deadlines, and locations scannable at a glance. Meet WCAG AA contrast and use readable body sizes.
3. **Rugged through restraint, not costume.** Suggest the backcountry through material honesty (paper-like surfaces, map and survey references, weather) and never through theme-park rustic.
4. **Generous wayfinding.** Every visitor type should find their path within one screen: submit, attend, get there, or watch past sessions. Use plain language with no jargon or insider shorthand.
5. **Durable and archival.** Design for a site that accumulates years of conferences, so past editions stay organized and dignified rather than piling up.

### Technical Constraints
- Jekyll + Minimal Mistakes, served from a subpath (`baseurl: /lookoutconference`). Put customizations in `assets/css/main.scss` / `_sass` overrides and `_includes`, rather than replacing the theme wholesale.
- WCAG AA; honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
